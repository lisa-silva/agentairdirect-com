Business Signal Intelligence FAQ

Is Business Signal Intelligence just schema markup?

No. Structured publishing is one part of the process. Business Signal Intelligence also addresses clear website content, business identity, services, locations, expertise, proof, customer questions, policies, first party content, and ongoing owner confirmed updates.

Does structured information guarantee that AI tools will cite or recommend my business?

No. No business can control how an independent search engine or AI system chooses sources or generates answers. Agent Air Direct helps build a clearer, more complete, and more supportable information foundation that modern systems can interpret.

Why does my business need this now?

Customers increasingly get information from search, maps, reviews, and AI tools before they contact a business. Clear, current, connected business information helps modern systems and customers understand what your business actually offers.
