Why this matters now

A strong Google presence doesn't carry over to the other AI tools.

Your Google Business Profile, Maps listing and Google reviews live inside Google. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot can't see that data. They read your website and the rest of the open web, and they piece your business together from whatever they find.

AI answers in sentences, not lists

There's no page two. The tool names a few businesses and describes them in its own words. If your facts are unclear, it guesses or leaves you out.

Each tool reads you differently

ChatGPT can describe you correctly while Perplexity has your old address and the wrong services. You won't know unless someone checks.

Scraps fill the gaps

Old directory listings, outdated hours, a page you forgot about. When your own website doesn't state the facts clearly, AI uses whatever else it can find.

Pricing Intelligence

Your customer shouldn't hear your price from AI before they hear it from you.

HVAC contractors started noticing the same thing: a customer calls about a water heater already holding a price an AI tool gave them. The number is wrong, and the tech walks into the job having to explain it.

AI tools answer pricing questions whether or not you've published prices. Without a source you approved, they piece a figure together from old pages, competitors and national averages.

The Pricing Passport

Pricing Intelligence builds a Pricing Passport for your business: one owner-approved record of how you price your work, published in a form AI systems can read.

  • Prices or ranges you approve, and what they include
  • Jobs that always need an on-site quote, marked that way
  • Effective dates, so old prices expire instead of lingering
  • Anything you haven't approved is never quoted

Questions

Frequently asked questions

We do well on Google. Why would we need this?

Google's data mostly stays inside Google. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot build their answers from your website and the open web. A business can be strong on Google and still be described poorly, or not at all, by the other tools.

Will this get us recommended by ChatGPT?

No one can promise that, and anyone who does is guessing. What we can do is make sure AI can find your business, read it correctly, and has clear, accurate reasons to choose it.

How is this different from an automated scan?

An automated scan reads your website code. The Business Signal Diagnostic looks at how your whole business reads, including identity, proof and consistency across sources, and comes with a ranked repair plan we can carry out.

Is this SEO?

No. SEO is about ranking in a list of links. This is about whether AI systems can understand your business well enough to describe it accurately.

Do we need to be technical?

No. You confirm the facts about your business. We do the rest and explain everything in plain English.

Where do you work?

Anywhere. We're based in San Jose, California, and work with businesses across the country remotely.

I run an agency. Can I offer this to clients?

Yes. Agencies use the same system through AgentAir at agentair.io.
